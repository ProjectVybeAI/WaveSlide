Features

Turn off what you do not use; fewer gestures means fewer surprises.

Sensitivity

Distances are in hand lengths: wrist to the middle knuckle. A swipe of 2 hand lengths is the same gesture at 0.5 m and at 2 m.

Timing

Held gestures fill the dial before they fire, one tick per 100 ms, so there is time to change your mind. Cooldowns stop one gesture from firing twice.

Recogniser

Rules are written by hand and need no data. The trained model learns the same poses from recorded sessions. Both read the same landmarks; switch any time.

Trained model available

Random forest, trained 12 Oct 2026 at 14:20 from 3 participants and 41,280 frames. Leave-one-participant-out macro-F1 0.94; rules score 0.91 on the same sessions. Compare them in the Evaluation lab.

Overlay

The small window that stays on top of your slides. If your laptop screen is mirrored to the projector, the audience sees it too.
